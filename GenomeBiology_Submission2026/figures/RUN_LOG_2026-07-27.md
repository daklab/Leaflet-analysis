## Results recorded today

### Reproducibility across random initialisations (A6)

Measured *between* fits differing only in seed, since the atlas has no ground truth. Three levels,
which fall off for a mechanical reason rather than a deficiency:

| level | agreement | why |
|---|---|---|
| Ψ (program profiles) | median matched *r* = **0.97** | estimated from all 92,894 cells |
| φ (per-cell loadings) | median *r* = **0.65–0.87** | each cell's φ uses that cell's reads alone, at ~13.5% coverage |
| Leiden clustering of φ | ARI ≈ **0.31** | discretising a continuous simplex compounds the noise |

Not artefacts: the partitions agree with sequencing-depth quintile at ARI 0.012, batch 0.056,
dataset 0.056.

### Program ↔ cell-type transfer (A7)

Mean φ per cell type, training vs held-out cells, same model (no matching needed). 12,000 held-out
cells, full coverage, 27 shared cell types. At *K* = 10:

| seed | overall *r* | per-program | per-cell-type | same top program |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 0.998 | 10/10 > 0.9 | 27/27 > 0.9 | **100%** |
| 1 | 0.999 | 10/10 | 27/27 | **100%** |
| 2 | 0.999 | 10/10 | 27/27 | **100%** |

*K* = 20 and 30 running under 19345951; `finish_a7.sh` builds the per-K figures and writes
`$MD/A7_transfer_summary.csv` when the array drains.

**Caveat carried into the Methods:** this is a cell-type-level measure. Averaging the cells within a
type cancels per-cell noise, so *r* = 0.998 must be read next to the per-cell median of 0.65–0.87,
not in place of it.

### Lineage relationships replicate (A8)

Each claim posed as an ordering between two cell-type profile similarities — matching-free and
comparable across *K*. Wilcoxon signed-rank on the nine paired gaps:

| claim | holds | mean gap (min) | *P* |
|---|---|---|---|
| excitatory types resemble each other > inhibitory neurons | 9/9 | +0.618 (+0.192) | 0.0039 |
| microglia resemble myeloid > macroglia | 8/9 | +0.547 (−0.072) | 0.0078 |
| B/T/NK resemble each other > myeloid | 9/9 | +0.188 (+0.003) | 0.0039 |

Separations widen with *K* (lymphoid gap 0.067 → 0.149 → 0.348 at *K* = 10/20/30); the single failure
is *K* = 10 seed 1, the same model with the weakest lymphoid separation. At *K* = 10 there are not
enough programs to resolve the immune compartment and which sub-lineages separate depends on the
initialisation; by *K* = 30 it is unambiguous in every seed.

### Transfer controls (A9) — what the agreement is worth

`r = 0.998` alone is a flattering statistic on this map (mostly near-zero entries, a few large ones).
Reported instead:

| metric | value |
|---|---|
| **relative error on the 78 entries with loading > 0.05** | **4.4%** (3.9–4.9 across seeds) |
| Spearman over the whole map | 0.987 |
| mean absolute deviation | 0.005 on entries averaging 0.100 |
| largest single disagreement | 0.098 |

Against the three reference points: sampling floor 0.9995, transfer 0.9984 (**cost 0.0011**), a
different seed's held-out map 0.7486, shuffled labels 0.2435. Held-out φ is not prior-dominated —
perplexity 2.37 vs 2.37 training, against 9.75 for the prior.

### Effective K (A10) — the main finding

Canonical correlations between two seeds' φ (rotation- and permutation-invariant) show a cliff.
Counting those above 0.9: **effective K = 4.7 of 10 (47%) and 10.0 of 20 (50%)**. The fraction is
stable, so the atlas has not saturated by K=20 and higher K buys real structure.

Two natural explanations were tested and **excluded**:

- *not coverage* — per-cell agreement by depth quintile is 0.833 / 0.919 / 0.808 / 0.912 / 0.853,
  flat over a 450-fold range;
- *not dead programs* — π is near-uniform (perplexity 9.75 of 10, 19.03 of 20), **zero** programs
  below half-uniform in any seed, sorted π reproduces at r = 0.92–0.94, and usage barely predicts
  reproducibility (lowest-usage half Ψ r = 0.972 vs highest 0.982; ρ = +0.25 to +0.38).

**Mechanism: the dictionary is stable, the per-cell encoding is degenerate.** Ψ reproduces at
0.96–0.98 for every program and π at ~0.93, but how a cell splits its mass across overlapping
programs is not unique. One mechanism accounts for the whole hierarchy — Ψ 0.97, cell-type mean φ ~4%
error, per-cell φ 0.65–0.87, Leiden ARI 0.31.

**Open question this raises.** `project_leafletfa_psi_prior` already suspected the junction-specific
Ψ prior of shrinking the K programs together. If so it is a candidate *cause* of the encoding
degeneracy, since more correlated programs are exactly what makes a per-cell decomposition
non-unique. That would sit in tension with the simulation result that the junction-specific prior
improves Ψ recovery under overdispersion (0.821 → 0.930). Testable without new atlas models:
`sim_benchmark.py` already fits both priors, so effective K can be computed for each arm against
known truth. Not yet done.

## Corrections made today

Recorded because each changed a number or a claim, and the wrong version is the kind that looks fine.

1. **A8's first formulation was wrong and said the biology did not replicate** (2/9, 4/9, 8/9). The
   tests were binary set comparisons — "same top-2 programs", "same argmax" — which get strictly
   harder as *K* grows, so a model resolving astrocytes and oligodendrocytes into separate programs
   was scored as a failure. Restated relationally, all three claims hold. The script's docstring
   records this so it is not re-derived.
2. **The lineage grouping was first read off a single heatmap by eye and was wrong in two places**:
   inhibitory neurons do not share the excitatory programs, and microglia do not load the macroglial
   program. Corrected against the numbers before it entered the Methods.
3. **`figure_program_celltype` was appended after the `__main__` guard**, so `python build_figures.py`
   would never have built it. Moved above and registered.
4. **Two documented paths in `REPRODUCE_ALL.md` were guesses that did not exist** — the A7 argv order
   and the φ cache filename. Both corrected against the script and the filesystem.
5. **`r = 0.998` was quoted as the headline for four exchanges before being checked against a scale.**
   It is inflated by the map's many near-zero entries; the defensible number is 4.4% relative error on
   substantive entries. The Methods now leads with that and cites *r* only against the sampling floor.
6. **Predicted the shared subspace would stay at ~5–6 dimensions as K grew** (i.e. that extra programs
   were unconstrained). It doubled to 10.0 at K = 20. The extra programs carry real reproducible
   structure; the earlier prediction was wrong.
7. **A7 sampled 12,000 of 39,812 held-out cells for no remaining reason.** The subsample existed to
   reuse A5's cached φ; once A7 became self-contained it was pure loss, costing ~7 cell types
   (kidney, pericyte, dendritic, lung, pancreatic ductal, neuron, VLMC — each has ≥50 held-out cells
   in total but not in 12,000). `a7_full_heldout.sh` re-runs at full size.
