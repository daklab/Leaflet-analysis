## 1. The headline is not "concordance". It is a trade.

**LeafletFA is far more conservative than LeafCutter under donor heterogeneity, and far less
sensitive.** Both halves are measured; neither should be reported without the other.

### Conservative — donor-split permutation null, identical splits, both methods

Both arms are the same cell type split by donor, so *every* call is a false positive.

| | events tested | false calls at nominal 0.05 | rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| **LeafCutter** | ~7,200 (its coverage filter) | **580** (median 559, max 1,323) | **7.7%** |
| **LeafletFA** | 24,475 | 33 | **0.14%** |

On the two cell types both methods could run: Heart cardiac muscle — LeafCutter 500 vs
LeafletFA 145; Heart endothelial — LeafCutter 717 vs LeafletFA 1.5.

The mechanism is specific and defensible: LeafCutter's Dirichlet-multinomial treats pseudobulk
samples as exchangeable with only DM overdispersion — **there is no donor random effect** — so
real between-donor variance has nowhere to go except into significance. This is the failure
mode Reviewer #1 asked about, and it is LeafCutter's, not ours.

### Not sensitive — the low-rank ceiling

Because `beta_j = sum_k dphi_k * psi_kj` with psi in [0,1], **|beta_j| <= |dphi|_1** is a hard
per-contrast ceiling. Across the 71 TMS contrasts |dphi|_1 spans 0.07 to 1.69 and **predicts
the effect-size slope at r = 0.87 (p = 7.6e-23)**. Median slope vs LeafCutter is 0.086–0.105
across seeds; AUROC for ranking LeafCutter-significant ATSEs is 0.62 (raw) to 0.66 (logit).

Concretely: Heart endothelial vs fibroblast has |dphi|_1 = 0.56, so the Fbln2 skip junction's
true dPSI of 0.90 is **above what the model can express at any psi**. LeafletFA still ranks
that ATSE 1st of 24,475 by prob_de — it finds the event, it cannot size it.

**Read together:** the same shrinkage that suppresses false positives suppresses true effect
sizes. That is a coherent, publishable characterisation of a low-rank model. It is not a
concordance claim and should not be written as one.

## 2. Where LeafletFA clearly wins

**Rare cell types (panel D).** Recall of the full-data marker set as cells are removed:

| cells per cell type | 1,737 | 869 | 435 | 175 | 88 | **36** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| recall | 1.00 | 0.98 | 0.97 | 0.94 | 0.91 | **0.85** |
| precision | 1.00 | 0.98 | 0.97 | 0.95 | 0.93 | 0.90 |

**85% of markers survive at 36 cells per cell type**, and |dphi|_1 is flat (0.939→0.945) down
the whole ladder — the group-mean phi is remarkably stable. LeafCutter's floor is >=4
pseudobulk samples x >=25 cells = >=100 cells, and in practice much higher.

*Caveat to state:* psi is held fixed from the full-atlas fit, so this measures robustness of
the **scoring** to cell count, not "train on 36 cells". That is the honest deployment claim —
LeafletFA needs no refit per contrast — but it is not a like-for-like retraining comparison.

**Real-data instance, not just downsampling:** `Lung_MONOCYTE` has 14 donors' worth of cells
but only **2 pseudobulk samples**, so LeafCutter cannot test it at any threshold. 34 of 96 TMS
tissue_cell_types clear the pseudobulk bar; 107 clear it at cell level.

**Held-out donor prediction.** Predicting held-out pseudobulk dPSI (scale-free R², so
attenuation is not penalised): LeafletFA mean **0.036** vs LeafCutter **0.021**, better on
**64.6%** of 900 splits, and 2.3x better on well-separated contrasts (0.076 vs 0.033).
**But both are tiny** — 2–8% of variance. At ~6 donors per arm neither estimator generalises
well; this refutes "attenuated therefore worse" but is not a strong positive claim on its own.

**Cost.** LeafletFA's permutation null needs no refit (unsupervised, so post-hoc label
permutation is free): 100 permutations x 6 cell types x 2 modes x 3 seeds in ~1 h each.
LeafCutter needs a full GLM refit per permutation: 60 refits ≈ 16 min each.

## 3. Corrections to things I reported earlier

- **"4 of 6 cell types give exactly zero false positives" was seed-specific.** Across all three
  seeds: seed 0 gives false positives in **all six**, seed 1 in two, seed 2 in five. Only Heart
  cardiac muscle is consistently bad (109/185/140). The null behaviour is **seed-dependent** and
  must be reported across seeds.
- **The max-detections FDR "fix" is a no-op.** FDP is provably monotone in rank (sorted by P
  descending, (1-P) is non-decreasing, so FDP(n+1)-FDP(n) = (x_{n+1}-FDP(n))/(n+1) >= 0), so the
  qualifying set is always a prefix and `len(where(...))` == `argmax(...)+1` identically.
  Verified over 600,000 randomised trials with adversarial ties: **zero disagreements**. A
  before/after curve for that change would show two identical curves. The defect that does move
  numbers is the post-hoc `|beta|>delta` filter, already absent from `differential_splicing_groups.py`.
- **The batch wrapper was never missing.** `differential_splicing_groups.py` has had
  `differential_splicing_junctions` since 2026-07-12, and its `group_delta_psi` already does the
  exact einsum optimisation, avoiding the [S,C,J] tensor.

## 4. Method choices, now evidenced

- **Statistic: logit TV.** Median AUROC 0.658/0.659/0.657 across seeds vs 0.622/0.628/0.622 for
  raw TV. Principled, not a fudge — LeafCutter's own GLM is logit-link. Compositional
  renormalisation does *not* help (0.620) because the simplex mostly holds already.
- **Posterior draws: S≈100 for effect sizes, S>=500 for anything FDR-related.** Mean TV
  correlates at r=0.999 from S=50 to 1000, but ATSEs saturating at `prob_de = 1.0` drop
  441→317 (−28%) — those report lfdr=0 as a resolution artifact, exactly in the calibration tail.
- **Seed reproducibility is strong.** Pairwise r = 0.87–0.93 across contrasts, versus the
  factor-level Jaccard of 0.51 that drove the reproducibility pessimism. **Group-vs-group was
  the right analysis to promote.**
- **TV is not size-neutral at fixed delta=0.1.** Fraction called scales 4.8x from 2-junction to
  6+ ATSEs (LeafCutter's scales 2.7x). I hypothesised simplex violation and **tested it — wrong**:
  renormalising leaves median TV unchanged. It is intrinsic to TV. The permutation null gives
  the per-size null distribution, so delta can be calibrated per size bin.
- **Beta-Binomial vs Dirichlet-multinomial.** A per-junction BB is exactly the DM's *marginal*;
  what is lost is the joint dependence the simplex induces. Violation scales with ATSE size:
  0.3% of 2-junction ATSEs violate by >0.05 vs 82% of 11+ junction ATSEs.

## 5. Outstanding

- `A28_sim_fdr_calibration.py` (job 19452303) — the **true** nominal-vs-realised FDR curve, on
  simulations with 31% true positives. The permutation null cannot produce one: under a global
  null realised FDR is 1.0 whenever anything is called, so it measures type-I error *volume*.
- Held-out R² is computed over all junctions including the majority with no signal; restricting
  to covered/tested junctions would raise both methods and is worth doing.
- The `delta` for the logit statistic needs deriving (0.1 is only interpretable on the PSI scale)
  before switching A26 over.
