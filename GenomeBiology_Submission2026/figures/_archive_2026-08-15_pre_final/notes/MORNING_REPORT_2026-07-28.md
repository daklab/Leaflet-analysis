## Does overdispersion explain the seed irreproducibility? (A13, simulation with known truth)

Same simulated design at three dispersion levels; only c changes. If c=2 (atlas-like) collapses
phi reproducibility while the Binomial arm does not, the atlas ~50% is an information ceiling
set by the data rather than a defect of the model or optimiser.
